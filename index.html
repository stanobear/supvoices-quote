More questions to ask
  • Who touches an invoice from arrival to payment? Shows hand-offs between accounts, ops and supervisors.
  • How do invoices arrive? Shared mailbox, post, supplier portals.
  • What happens when the amount doesn't match the voucher? Who decides, how long it waits.
  • How many invoices are waiting right now, and the oldest one? The backlog is the pain in July.
  • Have you paid a supplier twice, or paid more than the voucher, in the last year?
  • How do suppliers chase you for payment, and how often?
  • Do you get credit notes, deposits or pro formas? Credit notes count towards the allowance.
  • Where are invoices stored today, and could an auditor find one in a minute?
  • Do you use Microsoft 365 with SharePoint and Teams? Who in IT would connect it?
  • Which accounting system do you use? Xero is supported; others on request.
  • Does TourPlan already send supplier invoices to Xero? If yes, decide which system creates the bill, or you'll get duplicates.
  • Where are supplier bills approved today, and by whom? Steer towards approval in Supvoices.
  • How are suppliers paid: Xero batch payments, the banking app, something else?
  • Do people edit or delete bills in Xero after they're posted?
  • Which account codes, tax rates and tracking categories do supplier bills use? Any in other currencies?
  • Do you hire temporary staff in peak season for the purchase ledger?
  • How many separate companies process supplier invoices?
  • Who signs off on new tools, and what would they need to see?
  • When would you want to go live? Before the season is the usual answer.

Monthly subscription

The monthly fee is worked out from the allowance: platform fee, plus each invoice above the included number at the tier price. Invoices above the allowance are charged at the tier price for the next invoice, plus any overage uplift.

Xero posting is the standard add-on: approved bills and credit notes are posted to Xero, with a daily integrity check. Approval in Xero is custom work, built from a signed Integration Specification.
